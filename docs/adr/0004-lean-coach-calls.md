# Lean Coach calls with progressive disclosure

Every Coach call starts fresh with a compact brief: the position, the Engine's facts and a short summary of the conversation so far. It is never one long, growing Claude conversation. The Player Profile is structured data plus terse notes, and the Coach reads only the parts the task needs. A subscription's limits are measured in tokens, so in a long conversation every new turn would cost more than the last.

## Consequences

- Reviews cover the 3 to 5 key moments of a game, not every move.
- The app records Player Profile facts itself at no Claude cost: finished Lessons, and Move Grade counts per opening. The Coach adds at most one terse line at the end of a Lesson or Review, and only when it noticed something new.
- Claude is used for what the Engine can't provide: the qualitative "why". Expensive live features, such as Live Coaching, can always be switched off.
